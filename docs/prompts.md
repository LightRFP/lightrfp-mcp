# Example prompts

These are discovery scenarios, not assertions that every jurisdiction requires or currently offers each bond. Specify the actual requesting authority and jurisdiction where known. Never include applicant personal information.

## Starting or expanding a business

- “I'm opening a home healthcare agency in Florida. Find relevant bond products, explain how to distinguish them by requesting authority, and show catalog pricing where available.”
- “I'm starting a used-car dealership in New York. Find dealer bond options and explain the face amount, premium, term and recorded credit requirements.”
- “I'm forming an employment agency in Washington, DC. I've been told I need a $50,000 bond. Find the matching product and show the explanation and application links.”
- “I'm starting a freight brokerage. Find the relevant federal bond products and explain what this catalog can establish about price and preparation.”
- “I'm opening a business, but all I have is a request for a license bond. What information should I get from the licensing authority so you can find the right product?”

## ERISA and plan-related searches

- “Find ERISA fidelity bond products. Explain the stored amount options and pricing; don't infer my legally required coverage amount.”
- “Compare the returned ERISA bond options and show the Light RFP explanation page for each.”

## Contractors and bonding programs

- “My New York construction company wants a bonding program so we can bid on projects. Find contractor prequalification options and explain the stored application preparation topics.”
- “We want $2 million in aggregate bonding capacity. Explain the program discovery path without treating that limit as an issued bond amount or approved capacity.”
- “A project requires performance and payment bonds. Find relevant catalog products and explain what still requires application or underwriting review.”
- “I've been asked for a bid bond. Explain the available path, any recorded program requirements, and where to learn more.”

## Price, credit and next steps

- “What is the catalog premium for this matched bond, including recorded fees and the base term?”
- “Does the stored application evidence indicate credit authorization? Distinguish verified evidence from unknowns.”
- “Give me the explanation page first, plus the direct full-application link if the product and amount are confirmed.”

The connector does not search project/RFP opportunities, retrieve a customer's application status, or approve bonding capacity.
